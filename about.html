About Paws & Groom

Our story, our passion, our team.

We're Pet Lovers First

Founded with a simple belief: every pet deserves to be treated with love and respect. Our salon was built by groomers who are passionate about animals and dedicated to delivering the highest quality care.

We work with all breeds, sizes, and temperaments — including anxious pets. Our approach is always calm, gentle, and stress-free.

Based in Riga, Latvia, we're proud to be trusted by hundreds of local pet owners.

Our Values

Pet Welfare First

The comfort and wellbeing of your pet is always our top priority.

Eco-Friendly Products

We use cruelty-free, natural grooming products safe for pets and the planet.

Community Focused

We love our local Riga community and give back whenever we can.
