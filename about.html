About Grooming Studio Baron

Our story, our passion, our team — located in the heart of Rīga.

We're Pet Lovers First

Grooming Studio Baron was founded with a simple belief: every pet deserves to be treated with love, respect, and professional expertise. Our salon on Dammes iela 34 in Kurzemes rajons, Rīga has become a trusted destination for local pet owners.

We work with all breeds, sizes, and temperaments — including anxious pets. Our approach is always calm, gentle, and stress-free. In addition to grooming, we offer comfortable pet boarding so your furry friend has a safe home while you're away.

We are proud to be an LGBTQ+ friendly business — everyone and every pet is welcome here. 🌈
